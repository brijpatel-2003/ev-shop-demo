Who We Are

Mehsana's Trusted
EV Showroom

Dharmik Motors is Mehsana's dedicated electric vehicle showroom, proudly serving the community with genuine PEV electric three-wheelers, e-scooters, and e-loaders.

Founded by P Patel and rooted in Mehsana, Gujarat, we believe clean, affordable mobility should be available to every family. We stock both lead-acid and lithium-ion battery variants so customers can choose the performance and warranty level that fits their budget — with prices starting as low as ₹1,15,000.

Every vehicle comes with a manufacturer-backed battery warranty, and our local Mehsana team is always available for after-sales service and support. No need to travel to the city — we're right here in your neighbourhood.
